**[Background]**

After COVID-19, the price hike for logistics have become the major cost concern for manufacturing companies who are especially import major raw materials and export their products mostly.

**[Action took before setting up hypothesis]**

After market and suppliers comparison,  we found out several factors that have impacted on the shipping price including seasonal gap and suppliers’ pain-point, who want the regular amount of goods to ship.

However, there was no professional who have deliever predictions of market price of shipment, and recent information of newly opening ports.

**[HMW]**

How might we redesign the traditional route so that client can utilize recent close suppliers(shipment companies) based on their needs and region-oriented pain points?
