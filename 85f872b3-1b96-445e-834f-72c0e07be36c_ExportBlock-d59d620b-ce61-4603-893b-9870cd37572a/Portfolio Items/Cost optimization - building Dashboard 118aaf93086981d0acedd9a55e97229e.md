# Cost optimization - building Dashboard

Customer: Food manufacturing company
Done by: high price of imported goods due to COVID-19

## Analysis of cost optimization process and the role of dashboard

---

    

![image.png](Cost%20optimization%20-%20building%20Dashboard/image.png)

- Since there are various cost factors impacting the business, recategorizing cost levers including raw materials to overhead cost should be first and foremost
- By analyzing big data, figuring out major cost factors which gives fluctuation and financial burden to the business is important
- After analyzing and building a strategy to tackle those major cost factors, I built dashboard to check the progress of the project and communicate with the client
- in this article, I just illustrate the some of simplified version of dashboard that I’ve built for my client not to reveal client’s information

## LV0 - Dashboard A : Primary Dashboard

---

A n-tiered cost dashboard allows users to understand costs of the network and key drivers.

This dashboard illustrate the whole progress of project by factory location. Each graph shows raw material waste, Operational Efficiency, Labor productivity, Labor utilization

By comparing each site, figuring out why and how there are gap among factories should be tackled 

![image.png](Cost%20optimization%20-%20building%20Dashboard/image%201.png)

## LV1 -Dashboard B : cost summary

---

This dashboard shows the cost lever by each factories. Although the client is producing the similar product, cost impact may be differed by their productivity and the raw material waste management. Therefore, figuring out major cost levers, and detailed cost buckets being consists of each cost levers are important. 

![image.png](Cost%20optimization%20-%20building%20Dashboard/image%202.png)

Since the client have not gathered all the data although it was being accumulated on their SAP, this dashboard attribute to the project hugely due to the fact that managers became easy to compare and concentrate each factories producing status in real time.
