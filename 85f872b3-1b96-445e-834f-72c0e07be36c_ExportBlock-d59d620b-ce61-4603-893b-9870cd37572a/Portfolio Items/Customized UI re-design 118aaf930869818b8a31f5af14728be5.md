Customer: Client A in E-commerce industry
Done by: low conversion rate due to user unfriendly UI

## Customized UI re-design

---

Client is one of the major commerce company which both broadcasts the products and also expose them into mobile application. While the customers are changing their purchase medium from TV to mobile, client’s mobile application revenue was rapidly decreasing. Therefore, after analysis of application we have found out that the UI of application was bad so even I was disturbed to buy the product. Therefore, we suggested that we have to revisit the whole UI for the application based on the analysis of customer data. During the project we have coworked with client’s web designer and developers. 

The mode of work was 

1. segmenting and analysis based on customer data (My team)
2. suggesting sample of UI and rationale(My team)
3. discuss the suggested idea and evaluate the attractiveness & feasibility with my team and client

General overview to set up strategies for customized UI

1. **To lead cross-selling (for already active customers)** 

![image.png](Customized%20UI%20re-design/image%202.png)

- To lead the cross selling or to expand the size of sales bucket by customer, it is important to analyze the purchasing behavior

![image.png](Customized%20UI%20re-design/image%203.png)

- If 1st campaign was fail to lead immediate buying of customer, showing targeted review by others is also important to lead the revisit of the page and take a look the product once again

![image.png](Customized%20UI%20re-design/image%204.png)

- Until end of the payment, customers would continuously think about whether they stop purchasing behavior or not. Therefore, continual exposure to the other’s satisfaction and behavior would incentivize customers to proceed buying and other products as well
- By giving them limited time of discounted offer, the client may motivate customers to take a look other related products and lead them to buy the one.

1. **Targeting 5060 senior customers (major of the client)** 

![image.png](Customized%20UI%20re-design/image%205.png)

- based on the customer data and market research including competitor analysis, we’ve figured out that 5060 customers contribute to the sales the most. Therefore, giving more comfortable and attractive UX should be first and foremost to raise additional sales and strengthen brand loyalty
- Based on their purchasing behavior and demographic trait analysis, we suggested differently designed UI by individual customers

1. **Encouraging participation of customers for mobile contents**   

![image.png](Customized%20UI%20re-design/image%206.png)

- Those who usually enjoys mobile application want to feel enjoyment through live-commerce. Therefore, leading participation of them would increase the connectedness with the hosts, application and the customer which will be eventually extended to the revisit of purchasing page. Therefore, by one-way live-commerce, we suggested to have more entertaining functions so that more people have more touch point with the medium itself and the product
